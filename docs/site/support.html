Support

Turn it on

iPhone and iPad: open Safari, tap the aA or puzzle-piece icon in the address bar, choose Manage Extensions, turn on Sunglasses at Night, then allow it on all websites (or the sites you choose).

Mac: open Safari > Settings > Extensions, tick Sunglasses at Night and allow it on all websites.

A page looks wrong

  1. Open the toolbar popup and set This site to Never dark to confirm the extension is the cause, or try the other engine for that site.
  2. Tap Report a problem. It opens a draft email in your Mail app with the site's hostname, app version, system version and your settings. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

Remove your data

In the app, open Settings and choose Reset all settings. Delete single sites in the Sites list. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stores. The app keeps nothing on any server.
