Privacy Policy

Sunglasses for Safari · Last updated: [date of publication]

Who we are

Sunglasses for Safari is a free dark mode extension for Safari on iPhone, iPad and Mac, published by [publisher legal name and location, pending]. Privacy questions: hello@rarecreatures.app.

The short version

Sunglasses for Safari does not collect your data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no tracking. It never sends the pages you visit, their addresses, or your settings to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings: the mode (system, always, schedule, off), schedule times, look sliders, image options and similar choices. They are stored on your device in Safari's extension storage and in storage shared between the app and its Safari extension. They stay until you change them, reset them, or uninstall the app. Settings > Reset all settings in the app erases them.
  • Per-site choices: if you choose "Always dark" or "Never dark" for a website, or give it its own look, the website's hostname (for example example.com) and your choice are stored on your device. You can delete any of them in the Sites list, or use Reset all settings. Uninstalling removes them.
  • A copy of your settings for the app: so the app can show your settings and carry out "Reset all", the extension keeps a copy of your settings (including those hostnames) in storage shared with the app. It never leaves your device. It is replaced whenever your settings change and removed when you reset or uninstall.

Access to websites

To change how pages look, the Safari extension needs permission to run on all websites. While a page is open, the extension reads the page's styles and address in memory so it can compute darker colors and look up your rule for that site. This happens only on your device. Page content and addresses are not stored (apart from a hostname when you create a rule for it) and are not sent anywhere. You can restrict the extension to chosen sites in Safari's settings.

Loading a page's own stylesheets

Safari does not let an extension read some stylesheets that a page loads from another server. For those, the extension asks for the same stylesheet again from the same address the page already used. That request goes to the website's own server, never to us or a third party. It carries no cookies and no referrer, and is usually answered from Safari's cache. The only thing in the request is that stylesheet's address. The downloaded text is held in memory while it is needed and is not saved by us.

Reporting a problem

"Report a problem" opens a draft email in your own Mail app, addressed to hello@rarecreatures.app. The draft contains the website's hostname, the app version, your operating system version and your current settings. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your Mail app, and you can edit or delete anything in the draft first. If you send it, we receive your email address and what the draft contains, and we use it only to look into the problem and reply. Report emails are kept for [retention period, pending]. Ask us at the address above to delete yours.

What we do not collect

We do not use analytics, advertising, accounts, advertising identifiers, location, cookies, remote configuration or iCloud sync. There are no purchases.

Who else is involved

The app has no third-party services. Apple provides Safari and the App Store, and handles downloads under its own policies. The email service behind hello@rarecreatures.app receives only the emails you choose to send.
