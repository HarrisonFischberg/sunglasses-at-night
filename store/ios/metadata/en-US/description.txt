You open a link in bed and a bright white page lights up the room. Sunglasses at Night is a Safari extension that turns bright websites dark, so late reading is easier on the eyes.

DARK WHEN YOU WANT IT
• Match your device's appearance, stay dark all the time, or set a schedule that can run overnight.
• Turn it on or off from the extension's button in Safari. On Mac, Option-Shift-N toggles it too.

EVERY SITE, YOUR WAY
• Set a site to always dark, never dark, or follow your main setting.
• Give a site its own look with brightness, contrast, sepia and grayscale, or start from the Standard, Soft, Warm and Mono presets.
• If one part of a page looks wrong, point at it and pick a fix. It stays fixed on that site.

MADE FOR READING
• Page colors are rewritten rather than simply flipped, with text contrast in mind.
• Photos and videos are left as they are by default. You can dim them, or tell the extension never to invert them.
• Pages that are already dark are left alone, unless you ask for them to be darkened anyway.
• A second engine, Filter, is there for the odd site that looks broken. Switch per site, no reload needed.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
• Your settings and site rules stay on your device.
• Report a problem opens a draft in your Mail app. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

FREE
Free on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

GETTING STARTED
Open the app once and follow the steps to turn on the extension in Safari settings. Then allow it on the websites you want darkened.

Some pages may not look perfect in dark mode. If one doesn't, try the Filter engine or Never dark for that site, or use Report a problem in the extension.
