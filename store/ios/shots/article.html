Reading

I read for an hour before bed

It took me about a year to make the habit stick.

By Nora Lind · 6 min read

After dinner I wash up, leave my phone on the shelf in the hall and sit down with whatever I'm reading. Most nights I get through thirty or forty pages before I fall asleep.

Books are easy. Articles are harder, because I read those on a screen, and a bright white page in a dim room makes me squint. For a while I printed the long ones at work.

My sister told me to buy a brighter lamp. I tried that first.

Now I turn the page dark instead. The text is light gray on near black, and the lamp can stay where it is.

This essay first ran in our spring issue.

I still print the very long ones.
